- CT BuildFest 2.0 is a 30-hour hackathon on 30-31 October 2026 at CT Group of Institutions, Maqsudan, Jalandhar.
- There are 48 problem statements across AI, Cyber Security, Smart Education, IoT/Robotics, and Sustainability.
- Each team must have 3-6 members.
- Each team must select one problem statement ID.
- The selected problem statement cannot be changed after physical registration.
- Projects must be developed during the hackathon; pre-built projects are not allowed.
- Teams must bring their own laptops, chargers, devices, hardware, and components.
- Open-source libraries, datasets, and AI tools are allowed but must be credited.
- Submission requires project code, a working demo, short documentation, and a PPT.
- Maintain a GitHub repository or the provided submission format.
- AI and open-source usage must be mentioned in the README, code, PPT, and demo credits.
- Cyber Security projects must be defensive and tested only on permitted systems.
- Hardware projects must be assembled and developed during the hackathon.
- Evaluation includes problem understanding, innovation, technical implementation, impact, demo, ethics, privacy, and safety.
- Registration deadline listed in the rulebook: 22 October 2026.
- Coding officially starts at 11:00 AM on 30 October 2026.
